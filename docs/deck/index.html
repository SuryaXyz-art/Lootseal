2

Why transparency alone fails

Publish the whole table?

  • Spoils the surprise — players know exactly what's left.
  • Hands bots a map: buy only when the legendaries remain.
  • "The rares are gone, stop buying" games.

Publish nothing?

  • Nothing to check. Odds are a marketing claim.
  • Commit-reveal after the sale proves totals, but not that each draw was fair or that nobody peeked.

We need selective disclosure: prove the odds, hide the table. That's exactly what Midnight is for.

How Lootseal works

1 · CommitPublisher commits the hidden table and proves in ZK that it matches the advertised odds. Bond posted.
→
2 · BuyBuyer pays NIGHT and picks their own seed. The box is sealed in the queue.
→
3 · OpenA circuit draws from the hidden remaining supply with the committed seed + buyer seed, and encrypts the item to the buyer.
→
4 · Hold & tradeProve "top 1%" without revealing the item. Gift privately. Sell through escrow.
→
5 · AuditAfter the sale the table is opened and anyone re-checks it on chain.

Late box? Refunded.

Boxes open FIFO within a window — or the buyer claims price + penalty from the bond.

Nothing to snipe

Remaining supply is a salted commitment, re-committed every open. Every box carries exactly the advertised odds.

Private forever

The master seed is never revealed, so nobody can later publish who got what.

The product

Drop page

Proven odds, the catalog, a live queue, and a box that shakes, cracks and flips to your item when the proof lands.

Vault

Your items, decrypted locally from encrypted deliveries. Prove rarity, gift with a Lootseal ID, list on the bazaar, back up your keys.

Bazaar

Buy sealed items whose rarity is proven in ZK. Escrowed until the seller delivers — or refunded.

Studio

Design a drop with a live odds preview and launch it in guided steps. Run the queue, claim proceeds, reveal.

Audit & Verify

Every guarantee checked from public data. Rarity badges anyone can verify — no wallet needed.

Fulfiller

Opens boxes automatically within a block or two. Stateless: replays the hidden supply from chain data.

6

Built on Midnight's dual ledger

Public ledger

  • catalog, tier odds, price, deadlines
  • table / supply / seed commitments
  • request queue · item commitments · ciphertexts
  • badges · listings · NIGHT escrow & bond

Private state (provers only)

  • per-item counts · remaining supply · master seed
  • owner & encryption keys · decrypted items
  • encrypted in the browser (IndexedDB, passphrase)

Witness-checked work

Roll reduction and item pick are computed off chain and asserted in circuit — a lying witness can only fail. Secret indices never touch the ledger: vectors are read whole and selected with fold/map.

Engineering

15 + 1 provable circuits, 15 pure circuits shared with the browser. Staged deploy installs verifier keys via signed maintenance updates to fit block limits.

8

Honest threat model

Holds against the publisher

  • odds proven before sale · reveal must match
  • can't pick, skip or reorder boxes
  • late boxes refunded from the bond

Holds against everyone else

  • no peeking at the remainder — nothing to snipe
  • items and holders stay private
  • badges and bazaar sales are sound

Residual we state openly

A publisher buying its own boxes through other wallets knows the seed and can bias its own pulls (totals still hold, each try costs a box). Fix on the roadmap: threshold seed keepers — an independent auditor co-commits the seed, and every open needs both.
